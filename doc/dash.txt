Dash reimplementation
====================

Basic timed bursts share the ordinary movement pipeline. Spikes and
subpixel horizontal movement are not included.

src/dash.s owns tap direction/timer and burst velocity/timer.
Previous button state belongs to src/pad.s (buttons_prev). init_move
resets dash state through init_dash.

read_dash:

* Finds new presses with current buttons AND NOT previous buttons.

* Saves a first directional press and starts a 12-update countdown.

* Returns +1 (Right) or -1 (Left) for a matching second press before expiry.

* Returns zero otherwise. Holding a direction never counts as another press.

* An opposite press starts a new pair; both directions together cancel the
  pair.

* A completed pair is consumed. A and B do not affect detection.

There are no ground/air eligibility rules at this stage.

Build with make, then run python3 tools/test_dash_input.py (requires py65).

Shared movement pipeline
------------------------

update_move calls update_dash, set_velocity_x, set_velocity_y,
update_x, update_y, collision resolution, commit, and camera update.
There is no separate dash movement path.

set_velocity_x selects stored burst speed or ordinary walking input.
set_velocity_y selects vertical acceleration and applies jump impulses. During
a burst it selects vy = 0 and ay = 0. It does not integrate acceleration.
update_y always computes vy += ay, then next_y = y + vy; update_x computes
next_x = x + vx. Neither integration routine reads buttons or dash state.

Burst state and contacts
------------------------

update_dash consumes read_dash's direction to start an 8-update burst at
8 pixels/update. update_dash owns the countdown and publishes dash_velocity
as this update's burst speed (zero when inactive). Both velocity routines use
that speed as the active flag. On the final burst update the timer reaches zero
but the published speed remains nonzero until the next update or cancellation.
The starting update counts toward the duration; gravity resumes next update.
The jump latch prevents the zero vertical velocity from enabling a midair jump.
Input during a burst is discarded rather than queued.

* Floor contact cancels a burst, including contact with an existing support.
* Walls stop horizontal movement and cancel the burst.
* Another double-tap can start another burst in the same airtime.
* Jump input is suppressed during bursts; normal jump rules resume afterward.

Build with make, then run tools/test_dash_input.py and
tools/test_baseline_physics.py with Python and py65 installed.

Remaining review steps, each kept separate:

1. Safe movement substeps and world boundaries at burst speed.
2. One air dash per airtime, landing recharge, and ledge consumption.
3. Ground bursts, slope-following, and jumping out of ground bursts.
